Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@pilotcompass.app. For a bug report, please include the level, your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version, and what you saw. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Common questions

How does a level work?

Each level starts 30 seconds before the top of descent, cruising at FL370 on the arrival. You fly the descent with the MCP, the FMC, the speedbrake, flaps and gear, follow the ATC instructions, and the level is scored at the Landing Gate, 5 NM from the runway threshold. The beginner’s tutorial and the HOW TO screen explain the controls and the scoring.

Which levels are free?

Levels 1 and 2 (Lisbon and London Stansted runway 22) are free, with no ads. The full game unlocks every other launch level with a one-time purchase. New airports will come in themed packs, each bought once.

Does one purchase work on my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. Purchases belong to your Apple ID: on another device signed in with the same Apple ID, open SELECT LEVEL › UNLOCK and tap RESTORE PURCHASES.

I bought the full game but the levels are still locked.

Open the UNLOCK screen and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, with an internet connection. If that doesn’t help, email us with the date of the purchase.

Why did my level end as “Partially passed”?

All the gate criteria were met, but a published speed or altitude restriction was missed, or (with MONITOR CDA on) the continuous descent was broken. The debrief lists what was missed. Restrictions passed on radar vectors, or bypassed by an ATC direct-to, are not counted.

Why did ATC repeat an instruction?

ATC repeats an instruction when it is not being followed, with only the part concerned: altitude, heading or route, or speed. If it is still not followed, the level fails at the gate.

How do I change a value on the MCP?

Tap a window (speed, heading, altitude, vertical speed), then swipe up or down to change it. Tap the buttons to engage the modes.

I can’t hear the callouts or the ATC chime.

Check that the device is not in silent mode and the volume is up.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Settings and statistics are stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
