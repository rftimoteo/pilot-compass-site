Turn your experience into progress.

Pilot Compass is a personal self-assessment app for airline pilots. After each flight, log the conditions and rate yourself on the nine core competencies, in a Quick or Extended assessment.

  • See your competency profile, strengths and focus areas, and how they change over time.
  • Insights show when you perform best and when scores drop, from your own flights.
  • Before a flight, review what you usually meet at your departure and destination airports.
  • Works offline; your data is private to you.

Pilot Compass is a personal self-assessment tool, not an official training, licensing or regulatory record.
